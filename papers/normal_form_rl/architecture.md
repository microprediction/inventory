# Normal-form reinforcement learning for market making

Working notes upgrading P7 (and the forward half of P9) from "the symmetry as data
augmentation" to a full architecture. The controller is a sum of three layers:

    exact exponential controller + perturbative tangent controller + small learned residual

The exponential model supplies the coordinate system and most of the policy.
Learning is reserved for what is genuinely outside the theory: curvature of the
effective log-value curve, latent dynamics, adverse selection, and the global
integrability defect that curvature creates. The learner should never spend data
rediscovering the exact exponential symmetry; the theorem is hard-coded as the
reference controller and coordinate transformation.

The mathematics referenced throughout is the normal-form paper
(`../exponential_normal_form/exponential_normal_form.tex`): the effective log-value
$\Phi = -\log(G/G_\star)$, the exact translation of imbalance in the $(U,V)$
coordinates, the gauge $T_c = \Phi^{-1}(\Phi - \log c)$, the anchor operator $L_0$,
and the envelope transfer from log-survival deformations to $\psi$. The current site
demo (`docs/rl.html`, `docs/rl_core.js`) is the zeroth-order version of this design:
one Q-table in the balanced frame, the analytic $\delta(q)$ transformation, and a
small regime-specific residual. Its useful empirical observation, that the forward
learner partially re-centers itself under a misspecified transformation, carries over.

## 1. Learn an inventory potential, not two quote functions

An actor that outputs the buy and sell markups independently throws away the model's
strongest structural property: both quotes are generated by a single indifference
potential $\nu$. Parameterize the strikes as

$$K^{\downarrow}(x,z) = \epsilon + \frac{\nu(x+s,z)-\nu(x,z)}{s}, \qquad
  K^{\uparrow}(x,z) = \epsilon + \frac{\nu(x-s,z)-\nu(x,z)}{s},$$

with $z$ holding flow, win-curve, volatility, and adverse-selection state. Quote
extraction is then a one-dimensional optimization per side,
$m^\ast_\theta(K) = \arg\max_m (m-K)\bar F_\theta(m)$, performed analytically or by a
scalar solve. The learned object is the scalar field $\nu(x,z)$; the two quotes
follow. This enforces the cross-inventory compatibility (the edge identity
$K^{\downarrow}(x) + K^{\uparrow}(x+s) = 2\epsilon$) that a two-output actor will
generically violate.

For the deformation model $\Phi_\theta(K) = a + hK + \sum_j \theta_j \psi_j(K)$, the
potential decomposes as

$$\nu_{\theta,q}(x) = \nu^{(0)}_{1/2}\big(x;\, M(q)c,\, h\big) + \delta(q,h)\,x
  + \sum_j \theta_j\, \nu_j(x,q) + r_\omega(x,z),$$

where $\nu^{(0)}_{1/2}$ is the balanced exponential solution, $M(q)$ and
$\delta(q,h)$ are the exact multiplier and shift, each $\nu_j$ is a perturbative
response solved once from the linearized Bellman equation, and $r_\omega$ is the
learned residual. The exponential theorem makes the first two terms exact at
$\theta = 0$ for arbitrary carrying cost, so the residual is constrained to vanish
at the anchor. This is a nearly actorless system: learn a differential value, then
extract the control analytically.

## 2. Put the critic in the nonlinear normal coordinates

There is a stronger representation than subtracting the exponential $\delta(q)$.
For arbitrary $G$, with $u_\pm = \Phi(K^{\downarrow,\uparrow})$,
$U = (u_+ + u_-)/2$, $V = (u_+ - u_-)/2$, and $\ell(q) = \tfrac12\log\frac{q}{1-q}$,
the local enquiry Hamiltonian satisfies exactly

$$q\,G(K^{\downarrow}) + (1-q)\,G(K^{\uparrow})
  = 2 G_\star \sqrt{q(1-q)}\; e^{-U} \cosh(V - \ell(q))
  = G_\star\, e^{-\bar U} \cosh \bar V,$$

where $\bar U = U + \log M(q)$ and $\bar V = V - \ell(q)$. Imbalance disappears from
the local Hamiltonian for every positive smooth win curve; exponentiality is only
what makes the map from $(\bar U, \bar V)$ back to physical strikes affine.

The representation this suggests: the value network learns $\nu$ (global
integrability); the critic and replay buffer live in $(\bar U, \bar V)$, so
experience from every $q$ lands in one balanced normal frame; and the residual
critic learns only the failure of the local normal form to integrate globally
across inventory states, which is the defect functional of the paper. The
exponential $\delta$-shift used by the current demo becomes the linear
approximation to this exact nonlinear coordinate change.

## 3. Response functions replace per-regime tables

For each basis perturbation $\psi_j$, one linear solve at the anchor,
$L_0 \nu_j = -R_j$, gives a response function; the same anchor operator serves every
basis direction. The critic then has the form

$$Q_\omega(s,a,\theta) = Q_0(\bar s, \bar a) + \sum_j \theta_j\, Q_j(\bar s, \bar a)
  + \Delta Q_\omega(s,a,\theta),$$

with the residual indexed by win-curve shape rather than by an arbitrary regime
label. A per-$q$ residual table cannot extrapolate to an unseen imbalance or a new
markup distribution; the tangent representation can. Training on coefficients for
increasing and decreasing hazard should permit zero-shot interpolation to a Weibull
or logistic model whose coefficient vector lies in the same local span.

## 4. Robustness over hazard curvature, not arbitrary transition kernels

Online estimation gives $\hat\theta_t$ and a covariance $\Sigma_t$; the uncertainty
set is the ellipsoid $\Theta_t = \{\theta : (\theta-\hat\theta_t)^\top
\Sigma_t^{-1}(\theta-\hat\theta_t) \le \rho_t^2\}$ plus functional constraints such
as $\Phi'_\theta > 0$ on the visited strike range. The perturbation expansion makes
the inner adversarial problem nearly trivial: to first order,

$$\min_{\theta \in \Theta_t} J(\pi;\theta) \approx J(\pi;\hat\theta)
  - \rho_t\, \big\| \Sigma_t^{1/2}\, g_\pi \big\|_2,
  \qquad g_\pi = \nabla_\theta J(\pi;\hat\theta),$$

a sensitivity penalty on the poorly estimated curvature directions of the win
curve. At the action level, $a_t = \arg\max_a [\bar Q(s_t,a) -
\rho_t \|\Sigma_t^{1/2}\nabla_\theta Q(s_t,a)\|]$; second order gives a small
trust-region quadratic rather than adversarial simulation of entire MDPs.

This is deliberately not a state-action rectangular robust MDP. The shape parameter
$\theta$ is a global functional parameter shared across all states and quotes;
letting nature choose an unrelated win curve at each inventory-action pair would be
statistically incoherent and excessively pessimistic. The low-dimensional
perturbation structure is what lets the economically correct coupled uncertainty
stay tractable.

## 5. The exponential solution as a Bellman control variate

With $\nu_0$ the exponential-anchor differential value, train the critic on the
shaped residual reward

$$r_t^{\text{res}} = r_t - \bar r_0 + \nu_0(x_{t+1}) - \nu_0(x_t).$$

The potential term telescopes over any horizon to a boundary term
$\nu_0(x_T) - \nu_0(x_0)$, which vanishes in the long-run average for bounded
inventory, so policy ranking is unchanged. Under the exponential model the residual
Bellman target is approximately zero; under nearby models it is first order in the
perturbation. The learner estimates $\Delta\nu = \nu - \nu_0 - \sum_j\theta_j\nu_j$
rather than a full continuation value, which should sharply reduce TD variance in
illiquid settings with sparse fills. The methodological kinship is with linearly
solvable control: keep a tractable reference problem, learn a transformed value.

## 6. Online estimation of the perturbation

Enquiry direction is observed, so $q_t$ is easy to filter. The competing quote
distribution is seen through binary fills, $Y_t \sim \text{Bernoulli}(\bar
F_\theta(m_t))$. Model the raw log-survival as $-\log\bar F_\theta(m) = hm +
\sum_j\theta_j r_j(m)$ with constant and linear parts removed, fit from fills, and
push through $F_\theta \mapsto G_\theta \mapsto \Phi_\theta$ by the scalar markup
optimization and the envelope identity (to first order the optimizer's movement
drops out, so the push-through is an evaluation, not a re-solve). Suitable bases:
low-order orthogonal polynomials on the visited markup interval, monotone splines
for the log-survival, or a few interpretable directions (increasing hazard,
decreasing hazard, tail thickening, local crowding).

Robustness should be local. Weight the confidence geometry by the occupation
measure of the current policy,
$\langle\psi_i,\psi_j\rangle_\pi = E_{d_\pi}[\psi_i(K^{\downarrow})\psi_j(K^{\downarrow})
+ \psi_i(K^{\uparrow})\psi_j(K^{\uparrow})]$, so the uncertainty set is not
dominated by tails the policy never visits. The anchor also helps exploration:
around neutral inventory the exponential optimum fills at about $e^{-1}$, so
exploitation is already informative, and small quote dithers identify the local
hazard slope without visiting extreme fill-probability regions.

## 7. Minimax regret rather than unqualified max-min

Pure max-min reward invites the usual robust-control pathology: under enough
win-curve pessimism the safe policy quotes wide and barely trades. Prefer either
minimax regret,

$$\min_\pi \max_{\theta\in\Theta_t} \big[ J(\pi^\ast_\theta;\theta) - J(\pi;\theta) \big],$$

which is natural here because the anchor-plus-tangent controller approximates
$\pi^\ast_\theta$ well for every $\theta$ in the set, or a lower-confidence
objective $\max_\pi [\bar J(\pi) - \rho\|\Sigma^{1/2}g_\pi\|]$ subject to
operational constraints (inventory-tail probability, minimum fill rate). Either
formulation protects against model sensitivity without letting "do no business"
win mechanically.

## 8. Replacing the current demo learner

The demo's decomposition $Q_q = Q_{\text{shared}} + R_q$ is effective but not
identified: shared and residual components receive the same TD innovation, with no
centering condition saying which variation belongs where. Replace it with

$$Q_{\theta,q} = Q_0 + \theta^\top Q_1 + R_\omega(\theta, q),
  \qquad R_\omega(0, q) = 0, \qquad E_q[R_\omega(\theta,q)] = 0,$$

and a hierarchical penalty $\lambda_t \|R_\omega\|^2$ whose weight is set by
measured symmetry failure: when the between-regime residual is indistinguishable
from estimation noise the model collapses toward hard sharing, and when genuine
misspecification appears the residual is allowed to grow. This imports into the
forward problem the reliability-sets-the-pooling-dial idea already used in the
demo's inverse direction.

A concrete loop: estimate $q_t, h_t, \hat\theta_t, \Sigma_t$ from directions and
fills; update the anchor $\nu_0$ and the precomputed responses $\nu_j$; convert
replay into the balanced $(\bar U, \bar V)$ frame; train only the residual value
$\Delta\nu_\omega$ on the shaped TD target; extract candidate quotes analytically
from the potential; select the quote maximizing the perturbative lower-confidence
value or minimizing worst-case regret.

## 9. Theorem targets

Let $\varepsilon = \sup_{K \in I} |\Phi''(K)|$ after removing the best affine
anchor on the visited strike interval $I$. Under interior optima, uniform mixing,
and strong concavity of the quote Hamiltonian, the package to aim for is

$$\|\pi^\ast_\varepsilon - \pi_0\| = O(\varepsilon), \qquad
  J_\varepsilon(\pi^\ast_\varepsilon) - J_\varepsilon(\pi_0) = O(\varepsilon^2),
  \qquad
  \|\pi^\ast_\varepsilon - \pi_{\tan}\| = O(\varepsilon^2),$$

and, with enough smoothness, the sharpened value regret

$$J_\varepsilon(\pi^\ast_\varepsilon) - J_\varepsilon(\pi_{\tan}) = O(\varepsilon^4),$$

heuristically because the tangent controller leaves an $O(\varepsilon^2)$ action
error and local objective loss is quadratic in action error. The learning-side
counterpart is an oracle inequality: the adaptive learner performs, up to an
estimation penalty, as well as the best of hard symmetry pooling, perturbative
pooling, and fully separate regime learning.

The rate package is now certified numerically in the steady-state lattice model:
see `verify_tangent_rates.py` in this directory. Two structural facts make the
certificate exact rather than approximate. On the bounded inventory lattice with
one-sided Hamiltonians at the boundary states, the consistency solution satisfies
the average-reward Bellman equation with differential value $-\nu$ and gain
$g = s\,H_q(0)$, so it is exactly the optimal policy of the chain and its gain
matches a direct stationary-distribution evaluation (observed agreement 6e-17).
And the imbalance tilt passes through those boundary rows exactly, so the
exponential anchor remains exact on the bounded chain (residual 2e-16), with no
closure approximation.

Measured on the true Weibull family $k = 1+\eta$, $\eta \in \{0.16, \dots,
0.02\}$, with successive-halving ratios in brackets: exponential-controller
regret $O(\eta^2)$ [3.6, 3.8, 3.9]; anchor-potential regret $O(\eta^2)$
[3.4, 3.6, 3.8]; tangent regret $O(\eta^4)$ [14.5, 15.2, 15.5]; anchor action
error $O(\eta)$; tangent action error $O(\eta^2)$. The conjectured
$\varepsilon^4$ sharpening holds in this model. Magnitudes at $\eta = 0.16$, a
visibly non-exponential win curve: the fully exponential controller gives up
$4.5\times 10^{-3}$ per enquiry against an optimal gain of about $0.25$, while
the anchor-plus-tangent controller gives up $5.4\times 10^{-6}$, three orders of
magnitude less, from one linear solve on top of the anchor.

## 10. The first serious experiment

Build it as model-based robust control, not PPO or SAC: the environment model is a
one-dimensional response curve and the conditional quote is a scalar optimization,
so model-free continuous control would waste the structure. Train on exponential,
Weibull near $k=1$, logistic, gamma, lognormal, and mixtures, all represented
through common curvature coefficients; hold out entire named families.

Comparators: per-regime unconstrained RL; hard exponential equivariance; the
current shared-plus-residual learner; anchor plus first-order tangent responses;
tangent responses plus the robust sensitivity penalty; a fully unrestricted neural
policy.

The decisive tests are not in-sample P&L: held-out values of $q$; held-out markup
families; abrupt hazard-shape changes; inventory-tail risk; adaptation speed after
regime changes; worst-case and minimax-regret performance; and residual magnitude
as a diagnostic of structural failure.
