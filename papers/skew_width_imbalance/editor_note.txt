Subject: Corrected manuscript for [MS number] — On a Simple Relationship
Between Order Imbalance, Skew and Width in Over-The-Counter Trading

Dear Editors,

In follow-up work we found that Theorem 1 of our submission double-counted
a single factor. The balanced-overhead representation (overhead shifted by
gamma) and the fixed-overhead carrying-cost representation (cost multiplied
by M(q)) are equivalent frames, not simultaneous physical adjustments.
Consequently the submitted statement that physical quotes widen directly by
gamma, together with several dependent corollaries, was incorrect. The
underlying Hamiltonian scaling remains valid.

The attached corrected manuscript states the exact finite-lattice
equivalence in the fixed-overhead frame, as a bijection of solution sets.
After a skew translation, both directional fill rates are slowed by
D(q) = 2 sqrt(q(1-q)); equivalently, the balanced comparison uses carrying
cost M(q)c with M(q) = 1/D(q). The physical quote map is explicit, and
physical width equals the width of the balanced-at-M(q)c policy rather
than that width plus 2 gamma. The revision also makes explicit the
finite-inventory-lattice state space and the interiority and symmetry
conditions needed by the corollaries, and clarifies that the
constant-width nonlinear-skew inversion is only reduced form unless its
integrability condition holds.

The accompanying verification suite checks the principal algebraic
identities symbolically, confirms that the candidate policies are left
unchanged by Howard policy improvement to machine precision with
independent raw-quote searches corroborating, and uses interval-Newton
calculations to prove existence, local uniqueness, and interiority in the
stated certificate boxes and over a stated interval of imbalance values.

We preferred to send the corrected manuscript now rather than have
referees spend time on statements we know to be wrong, and we apologize
for the inconvenience.

Sincerely,
