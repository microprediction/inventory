Subject: Corrected manuscript for [MS number] — On a Simple Relationship
Between Order Imbalance, Skew and Width in Over-The-Counter Trading

Dear Editors,

In follow-up work we found that the statement of Theorem 1 in our submission
conflates two equivalent representations of a single correction: the widening
of the non-discretionary width by gamma and the multiplication of the carrying
cost by M(q) = e^{h gamma} are the same factor read in two frames, not
simultaneous adjustments. The proof and the accompanying numerical certificate
are unaffected, as are all results concerning the skew; what changes is the
interpretation of the width response in Corollary 2 and Section 6, which is in
fact the balanced problem's convexity response to the multiplied carry. The
attached corrected manuscript amends the statement accordingly and adds the
closed leading-order form of the width response, Delta C(0) = gamma h C(0) /
(2 - h C(0)), together with a short numerical certificate verifying both the
corrected statement and the new formula.

We preferred to correct this now rather than during review, and apologize for
any inconvenience.

Sincerely,
Peter Cotton
