Twin dealers

The commutative square of the imbalance symmetry, running live: one inventory path, two clocks, quotes a constant apart.

Both problems below are solved numerically and independently; the square commutes to solver precision (first tile). The two dealers then trade side by side, coupled: they share one embedded jump chain — the same inventory path, trade for trade — while the imbalanced dealer's calendar clock runs slower by exactly D(q) = 2√(q(1−q)). Her quotes sit a constant δ nearer the sellers at every moment; her width equals her twin's at every moment; and the carry each pays per transition is identical — the multiplier M(q) = 1/D(q) is the slowed clock seen from the cost side. That, and nothing else, is what one-sided flow does after the skew is granted.

Look for: two mids a fixed distance apart with identical spreads; the trades-per-time ratio settling on D(q); the carry-per-transition ratio pinned at one; and the two occupancy histograms, one per clock, drawing the same shape.
