Mind the gap

The two symmetries

On the medium level the optimal gap is the easy level's optimal gap at carry M(q) a, shifted toward the sellers by δ = (w/2) log(q/(1−q)). Both policies are solved independently and the deviation tile reports the difference. A flat book still skews: the dashed gap is off centre before anything has been caught.

On the hard level the fair value is a martingale and the bar is in absolute price, so the market drifts away from the quotes. Quoting relative to the fair value recovers the medium level.

The mark-to-market term Σ x·dF has zero mean and changes no decision. A dealer who dislikes its variance pays λσ2x2/2 per unit time for it, and that is the back pain: the quadratic carrying cost is the price risk.

Model: market width w = 1, adverse selection ε = 0.25, inventory capped at ±6, mean inter-arrival one second. The default carry lies within the theorem's interior region on this lattice. The benchmark policy is policy iteration from a flat cold start (fourcorners_core.js); the game logic is game_core.js.
