Demos

Live, in-browser computations backing the papers on this site.

  • The imbalance symmetry
    The imbalanced and balanced market making problems are solved numerically and independently; after the skew shift δ and the carry multiplier M(q), one lands on the other to within 10−13. The dots on the line are the theorem.
  • Optimal and sub-optimal market makers
    Three dealers face the same enquiry stream and the same competing quotes: the optimal policy, constant-width linear-skew with no flow term, and no skew at all. The flow term is worth a few percent of profit; ignoring skew entirely is ruinous.

More demonstrations will appear here as the program's papers are written up.
